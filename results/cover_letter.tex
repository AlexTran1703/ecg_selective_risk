Dear Editor,

Please consider our manuscript, “Reader Disagreement Identifies Residual
Diagnostic Risk in Automated ECG Interpretation,” for publication as a Short
Communication in the Journal of Electrocardiology.

This secondary analysis of the CODE datasets examines whether reader
disagreement identifies diagnostic difficulty that persists after
uncertainty-based referral of automated ECG interpretations. Although referral
of lower-confidence ECGs reduced the overall diagnostic error burden, ECGs
with initial reader disagreement retained substantially greater error risk.
The findings highlight information about diagnostic reliability that is lost
when multiple expert interpretations are collapsed into a single adjudicated
reference label.

The manuscript is original, has not been published previously, and is not
under consideration elsewhere. I have reviewed and approved the manuscript
and take responsibility for its content.
