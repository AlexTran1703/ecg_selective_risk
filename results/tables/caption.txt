Figure 1. Uncertainty-based referral and cardiologist disagreement among model errors, CODE-test (827 ECGs x 6 diagnoses = 4962 diagnosis-record pairs). (A) Macro selective risk against coverage over q in [0.50, 1.00], with 95% ECG-level bootstrap interval; coverage denotes the proportion of predictions retained, so 1 - q is the referral fraction and q = 0.50 corresponds to 50% referred. Risk-coverage curves were computed over the full range q in (0, 1]. (B) Patient-level analysis of the 827 CODE-test ECGs, each from a distinct patient; an ECG is classified as disputed if the two cardiologists disagreed on any of its 6 diagnoses and as erroneous if the model was incorrect on any. Left, reader disagreement among all ECGs (33 of 827) and among ECGs containing any model error (11 of 61), a 4.5-fold enrichment. Right, model error among ECGs with complete reader consensus (50 of 794) and with any disagreement (11 of 33), risk ratio 5.3. Points are percentages and error bars are 95% ECG-level bootstrap intervals. The diagnosis-level analysis is reported in the text.
