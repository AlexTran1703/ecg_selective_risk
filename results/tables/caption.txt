Figure 1. Uncertainty-based referral and cardiologist disagreement among model errors, CODE-test (827 ECGs x 6 diagnoses = 4962 diagnosis-record pairs). (A) Macro selective risk against coverage over q in [0.80, 1.00], with 95% ECG-level bootstrap interval; coverage denotes the proportion of predictions retained, so 1 - q is the referral fraction and q = 0.80 corresponds to 20% referred. Risk-coverage curves were computed over the full range q in (0, 1]. (B) The same referral policy, with retained ECGs split by whether the 2 cardiologists disagreed on any of the 6 diagnoses. Both curves use the single global confidence ranking of (A); strata are formed only after retention, so coverage means the same thing in both panels. At full coverage, any model error occurred in 11 of 33 ECGs with any disagreement (33.3%) versus 50 of 794 with complete consensus (6.3%), a risk ratio of 5.29 (95% CI, 2.70 to 8.85). Shading in both panels represents 95% ECG-level bootstrap intervals. The lower strip of (B) shows the risk ratio between the 2 strata at each coverage, with each ratio formed within the bootstrap replicate rather than from the limits of the 2 risk curves, and a reference line at 1. It Below 90% coverage fewer than 15 disagreement ECGs remain and the lower confidence bound reaches zero, so the ratio there should be read as unstable rather than rising. Disagreement ECGs retained fall from 33 at full coverage to 6 at 80% coverage. RR = risk ratio.
