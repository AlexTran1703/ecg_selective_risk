Reader disagreement occurred in 33 of 4962 diagnosis-record pairs (0.67%) and was concentrated in 1dAVb (14 disagreements). Disputed labels showed greater decision uncertainty than consensus labels (delta U = 6.045 (5.442 to 6.409)). Uncertainty-based referral reduced macro classification risk from 0.012 at full coverage to 0.000 at 50% coverage. Before referral, disputed labels accounted for 14.5% of all errors against 0.7% of predictions, an enrichment of 21.8 (10.1 to 34.8)-fold; referral then removed nearly all error (62 residual errors at full coverage to 0 at 50%), leaving too few events to test whether the enrichment persists.
