Table V. STM32F411 deployment at 100 Hz in int8 (128 KB SRAM, 512 KB Flash). Flash and RAM are totals including the generated ST Edge AI runtime, not weights alone. Latency is measured on the part: each model was built into a bare-metal firmware, flashed over SWD and timed with the DWT cycle counter at 100 MHz over 32 runs after a warm-up, and the median is reported. Inference RTF is that latency over the 10 s acquisition window; it excludes preprocessing, which is performed off-device. Practical fit is assessed against a reserved budget of 448 KB Flash and 112 KB SRAM, leaving 64 KB and 16 KB for application firmware, drivers, acquisition buffers and a bootloader. All six models are physically smaller than the part and all six ran on it.
