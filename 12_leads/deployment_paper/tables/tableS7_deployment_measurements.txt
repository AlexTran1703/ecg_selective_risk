The last six columns are the quantisation comparison plotted in Figure 5(a-b), on identical records: dAUPRC is int8 minus float32 discrimination, dE-AURC is int8 minus float32 selective reliability, where a negative dE-AURC means the confidence ordering was not degraded. Latency excludes preprocessing, which is performed off-device. Practical fit reserves 64 KB Flash and 16 KB SRAM for application firmware on top of the measured totals.
