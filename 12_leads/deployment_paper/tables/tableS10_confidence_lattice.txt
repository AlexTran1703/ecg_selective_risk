Supplementary Table S10. The int8 confidence lattice. Levels is the number of distinct confidence values across a held-out fold; in float32 this is of the order of one per record, and after int8 quantisation it is a few dozen. Because a selective threshold can only fall between adjacent levels, the attainable coverages are the cumulative sizes of the tied blocks. The last three columns report how close a deployment can get to a nominal 90% operating point: across the 24 model-by-source folds, 20 miss it by more than two percentage points and in 8 the nearest alternative to withholding the least-confident tenth is withholding nothing at all. Discrimination survives quantisation; the resolution of the confidence ranking does not.
