Table 1. Composition of the four harmonised ECG sources. Counts are records retained after harmonisation to the 13-label space; records carrying no label inside it were dropped before any splitting. Patients are unique within a source, so the ratio to records shows that only PTB-XL repeats patients (17,928 patients over 20,487 records); identifiers are not comparable between sources, so the Total row sums within-source counts. Sex is normalised across sources, which record it differently; age is the median over records where it is present. Labels per ECG is the mean number of positive labels. The random-ranking AP reference is the macro-average of per-class prevalence, given so that Table 3 can be read against something other than zero; it is a reference, not a bound, and a model can score below it. NSR is not coded comparably across these four sources and its prevalence should not be read as a population difference. Inputs are 10 s, 12 leads, decimated to 100 Hz.
